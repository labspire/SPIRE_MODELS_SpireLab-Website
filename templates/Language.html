Language Data Coverage

SPIRE LAB maintains large-scale speech and text corpora across 9 Indian languages to support research and development in Automatic Speech Recognition, Language Identification, Dialect Identification, and Text-to-Speech systems. Below is a summary of the primary corpus backing our language coverage.

SYSPIN_S1.0 Corpus

A TTS Corpus of 900+ hours in nine Indian Languages

SYSPIN_S1.0 is the TTS corpus built as a part of the SYSPIN project at SPIRE lab, Indian Institute of Science (IISc) Bangalore, India. It is the current largest TTS corpus comprising more than 47 hours of single speaker's speech (1 male and 1 female voice artist per language) in each of nine Indian languages such as Hindi, Bengali, Marathi, Telugu, Bhojpuri, Kannada, Magahi, Chhattisgarhi, and Maithili. This corpus is unique in terms of duration per speaker and the variety of domains covered in the process of preparing the text in this corpora unlike existing TTS corpora in these languages. This corpus allows research and development in TTS including multi-lingual learning on studio-quality audio from multiple speakers, and different scripts for various languages, with part of the corpus having parallel sentences for speakers in a language.

When this corpus is used, please cite the following reference:
Abhayjeet et al. 'SYSPIN_S1.0 Corpus - A TTS Corpus of 900+ hours in nine Indian Languages', 2025.
