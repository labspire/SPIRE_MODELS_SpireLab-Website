About SYSPIN Project

SYSPIN (Synthesizing Speech in Indian Languages) is a foundational initiative from SPIRE Lab, IISc, aimed at developing studio-quality Text-to-Speech (TTS) corpora and advanced AI models across major Indian languages to shape inclusive and accessible voice technologies.
