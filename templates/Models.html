Three Core AI Pipelines Powering SPIRE LAB

Available Model Pipelines

Below are the three core deep learning models developed at SPIRE LAB, each fine-tuned for a specific speech intelligence task across 11 Indian languages.

1. ASR Model

Automatic Speech Recognition engine fine-tuned to capture native multi-dialect acoustics, converting spoken audio directly into clean transcription text.

View Details →

2. Language Identification (LID)

High-fidelity acoustic frontend engineered to detect and classify which of the 11 supported languages is being spoken in real time.

View Details →

3. Dialect Identification

Granular classification model trained to distinguish regional accents and geographic phonetic variations within a single language.
